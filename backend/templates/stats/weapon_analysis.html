Grenade Impact
Estimated
Grenade kills are inferred: CS2's Game State Integration feed has no killfeed, so a kill is read from inventory and ammo changes rather than a kill event. A kill counts as a grenade kill only when a grenade was thrown within its fuse/burn window and no shot was fired in that moment. Multi-kills in a single tick are high-confidence; a lone inferred kill is a softer estimate. Throw counts, by contrast, are exact.
